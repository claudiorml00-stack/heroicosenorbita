The Earth Information Jukebox

About the Project

We are Heroicos en Órbita, a team of high school students from Tacna, Peru, passionate about technology, robotics, and innovation. We are excited to join the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge and develop creative solutions.

We chose The Earth Information Jukebox to make Earth science accessible to everyone. Our project transforms NASA's Earth visualizations into interactive sounds, helping people with visual impairments explore our planet independently and discover its environmental challenges.
